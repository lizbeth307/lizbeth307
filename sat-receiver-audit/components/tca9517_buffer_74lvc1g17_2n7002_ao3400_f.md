- **Equivalence proof:** Every figure below is from a datasheet I checked myself.
  
  TCA9517DR vs TCA9517DGKR: same TI datasheet (SCPS242D) and identical electrical tables.
  - VCCA 0.9-5.5 V, VCCB 2.7-5.5 V
  - B-side VOL 0.45/0.52/0.60 V
  - VILC 0.4 V typ
  - ESD 5500 V HBM / 1000 V CDM, MM 200 V
  - I2C pins high-Z and 5.5 V tolerant when unpowered
  - tPLZ B->A max 250 ns; -40 to 85 C
  - Only the package differs. SOIC-8 has a lower thetaJA (133.6 vs 187.6 C/W) and is easier to hand-solder.
  - DigiKey has 8,405 of the DR in stock but only 1 of the DGKR.
  
  SN74LVC1G17 (TI, kept):
  - Hysteresis 0.51-0.83 V at 3 V
  - II +/-5 uA, Ioff +/-10 uA
  - +/-24 mA at 3 V
  - At $0.15 qty-1 at DigiKey it is already the cheapest genuine option.
  
  2N7002,215 (kept; cheapest at DigiKey, $0.18):
  - 60 V, VGS(th) 1-2.5 V, RDS(on) <= 5.3 ohm at 4.5 V/75 mA
  - IDSS <= 1 uA at 48 V, IGSS 100 nA
  - In the roles left to it (P-FET gate divider about 0.6 mA, J21 PMIC key and LC76G RESET at uA) a 3.3 V gate is sufficient.
  
  AO3400A (AOS, kept; datasheet Rev 3.1):
  - 30 V, VGS(th) 0.65-1.45 V
  - RDS(on) < 26.5/32/48 mohm at 10/4.5/2.5 V
  - IDSS 1 uA, IGSS 100 nA
  
  Discrete relay driver vs TBD62003A-class IC. Coil data from the Omron/Aratas G6K-2F-RF datasheet: DC5 coil 21.1 mA, 237 ohm (+/-10%), must-operate 80% max = 4.0 V at 23 C, about 100 mW.
  - Drop: <= 21.1 mA x 48 mohm = 1 mV, vs <= 24 mV for TBD62003A (RON 1.14 ohm max) and 0.7-1.1 V for ULN2003.
  - Hot margin (my model, assuming the copper tempco scales must-operate): about 4.6 V must-operate at a 60 C coil. 4.75 V - 1 mV still clears it; a Darlington driver fails.
  - Turn-on: VGS(th) max 1.45 V vs a 2.5 V min input for TBD62003A, so more margin from the 3.3 V MCP23017.
  - Power-up OFF: the 100k pull-down keeps both coils de-energised while the MCP is tri-stated or unpowered. The HF+ therefore defaults to 50 ohm, the receive-only terminated state.
  - Flyback: BAT54C, VR 30 V, IF 200 mA, IFRM 300 mA, against 21 mA, clamping the drain to about 5.4 V.
  - Disclosed paper losses: 30 V vs 50 V output rating (5.5x margin over the clamp), no rated input ESD (so 100 ohm series gate resistors are added), and 4-6 extra placements.
  
  AO3400A instead of 2N7002 on the spare fan path: a 2N7002 has no RDS(on) spec below 4.5 V and VGS(th) up to 2.5 V (2.75 V at -55 C), so 3.3 V drive cannot guarantee fan current. The AO3400A is specified at 2.5 V gate drive.
  
  BAT54C vs BAT54 (Nexperia BAT54C v.6): identical per-diode limits.
  - VF <= 240/320/400/500/800 mV at 0.1/1/10/30/100 mA
  - IR <= 2 uA at 25 V; Cd <= 10 pF; trr <= 5 ns; Tj 150 C
  - Pinout: pin 1 A1, pin 2 A2, pin 3 common K.
  - Every group in STACK is common-cathode: the button OR (cathodes at the grounded-button node; anodes at #2813 A, J21 pin 3 and GP11), the supervisor OUT/GP13 OR (cathodes at #2813 OFF), and the coil and fan flyback diodes (cathodes at +5 V).
  - Disclosed: Ptot 250 mW is shared per package (the load is <= 8 mW), and one package now carries two paths.
  
  General: no Linux driver is involved. labd still sets GPB2/GPB3 high = coil on, so nothing changes in software. The hard rules are unaffected.

- **Conditions or tests:** A) Relay driver bench test, on the G6K board with a bench supply set to 4.75 V:
  - Coil voltage >= 4.70 V with each coil on.
  - Clean pickup and release with the relay heated to about 60 C.
  - Drain clamp <= 6 V at turn-off (scope).
  - Both coils de-energised (HF+ on 50 ohm) through 20 cold boots, with MCP #1 both unpowered and tri-stated.
  - LiteVNA re-check: >= 35 dB isolation at 162-260 MHz and <= 0.3 dB loss from 10 kHz to 260 MHz.
  
  B) Spare fan path: with the Pico in reset, pin 33 high must run the fan at full speed, with AO3400A VDS < 50 mV.

  E) LCSC route only: spot-test one AO3400A per strip. VGS(th) at 250 uA must be 0.65-1.45 V, and VDS at 1 A with VGS 2.5 V must be <= 48 mV. Reject the strip on failure.
