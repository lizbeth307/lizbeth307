- **Equivalence proof:** Each spec below is listed as B&W 4000 vs Peli 1450, with its source. The B&W figures come from the B&W booklet 'outdoor.cases 08/2017' and the 2016 product overview (both manufacturer PDFs, served by Astroshop). The Peli figures come from the Peli 1450EU datasheets (RS docs, both versions read).
  
  BETTER OR EQUAL
  - Interior: 385 x 265 x 165 mm vs 374 x 260 x 154 mm. Larger in every axis, so the left/centre/right layout and the ESD-mat fold test carry over.
  - Base and lid depth: base 120 vs about 110 mm (Peli 15.4 minus 4.4 cm lid); lid 45 vs 44 mm.
  - Volume: 16.6 vs 15.0 L.
  - Empty weight: 2.3 kg spec vs 2.5 kg. The PAConsult lab weighed three empty Type 4000 units at 2.154-2.161 kg.
  - IP67: both. In the B&W lab immersion test (MIL-STD-810G 512.6 Proc I, 1 m, 30 min) no water got in.
  - Pressure valve: automatic on both. B&W calls it an 'automatisches Druckausgleichsventil'; Calumet says the same.
  - Shell: polypropylene on both.
  - Climatic, shock and vibration: B&W has a published third-party test report, PAConsult 17-8981 (2017). Temperature shock -33/+63 C, 40 g/11 ms shock, random and loose-load vibration: no damage.
  - Test claims: B&W claims STANAG 4280, DEF STAN 81-41 and ATA 300 (certificates not published). Peli claims MIL C-4150J and Def Stan 81-41 (no report published).
  - Dividers: included in the RPD price. The Peli 1455 is bought separately.
  - Seller: an EU commercial seller, so the 14-day withdrawal right applies.
  
  WORSE OR UNPROVEN (none is traded silently)
  1. Latch retention, the load-bearing gap. In B&W's own MIL-STD-810G 516.7 Proc IV drop test (1.22 m, 26 drops, empty case), the latches opened halfway on face 3. On face 1 the case OPENED COMPLETELY. On an unconditioned case, one latch opened halfway on both drops. The datasheet itself advertises 'leicht zu oeffnende Schloesser' (easy-to-open locks). The specimen secured 'with Splint' (a pin; the report does not say where it was fitted) passed all 26 drops with no opening. Peli publishes no drop report, but none shows its double-throw latches failing. The B&W is therefore not-worse ONLY with the latches or eyelets secured.
  2. Temperature range: -30/+80 C vs -40/+87.8 C (2026 Peli sheet; the older sheet says +98.9 C). This does not bind: the Li-ion bench contents tolerate a narrower range, and the B&W passed a -33/+63 C temperature shock.
  3. Guarantee: 30 years (manufacturer booklet) vs Peli lifetime.
  4. Padlock eyelets: 7 mm with no stainless protectors stated, vs 7.9 mm with stainless protectors.
  5. Hinge-pin material: unpublished, vs Peli stainless.
  6. Exterior: 420 x 325 x 180 vs 409 x 331 x 175 mm (+11 mm long, -6 mm wide, +5 mm tall). It still fits as checked baggage.
  7. Origin: B&W also runs a plant in Jiaxing, China (booklet address list), and the 4000's origin is unverified. The Peli 1450EU is made in Germany.
  8. Weight with RPD: 2.3 kg vs 2.95 kg between retailers (conflict).
  
  COMPATIBILITY
  The case is passive: no interfaces, drivers or rails. The hard rules are unaffected. The travel rules (D14) are unchanged: Li-ion items go in the cabin.
